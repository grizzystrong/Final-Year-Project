One trust score, four independent factors

No single signal decides. A device must look right on identity, behavior, history and context at the same time.

trust = 100 × identity0.18 × behavior0.47 × reputation0.17 × (1 − context risk)0.18

A weighted geometric mean means one failing factor drags trust down instead of being averaged away. Trust then falls fast and recovers slowly, and more critical devices are penalised harder.

Method & data transparency

  • Data. NSL-KDD, a public network-intrusion benchmark, replayed through a simulated fleet of 12 IoT devices. It is not a native IoT packet capture.
  • Synthetic telemetry. Request rate and payload size are generated per device type and perturbed by attack family. They are synthetic, not captured packets.
  • Identity. Credential age, authentication-failure and privilege signals, traffic fingerprint against the device type, and step-up challenge results. Challenge outcomes are part of the simulated environment; the engine never sees ground-truth labels.
  • Swap-in. The trust and policy engines are dataset-agnostic; N-BaIoT, TON_IoT or CICIoT2023 can replace the loader.
